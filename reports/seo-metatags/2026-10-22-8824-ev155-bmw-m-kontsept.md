### Recommendation
**Focus keyphrase:** `BMW M3 electric`
- It is the only phrase here with measured Bulgarian demand that this page can
  honestly satisfy: 30/month, with a June spike to 110 that lines up with this
  episode's own news.
- The hosts spend a long stretch reading the concept as the future electric M3,
  so the page answers the query.
- `bmw m3` (2,900) is deliberately **not** the target. The intent is the petrol
  car, the page cannot satisfy it, and BMW's own pages would own it.
- No cannibalisation: `/wp/v2/search` for "BMW M3" returns only old EV reviews
  (BMW i4 M50, iX3) and unrelated posts, and nothing on the site targets an
  electric M3.
- **Judgement call:** the keyphrase is in Latin script while the page is
  Bulgarian. It matches how the autocomplete tree is searched here, and
  `BMW електрическо M3` has no volume data at all. If you prefer a fully
  Bulgarian keyphrase, `BMW електрическо M3` is the fallback.
- **Caveat for Phase B:** BMW never called the concept an M3 in the news item.
  The hosts do. The prose must attribute the M3 reading to the hosts, not to BMW.

**Secondary:** `bmw neue klasse m3`, `bmw m концепт`, `bmw electric m3`

This is a content-gap page (14 owned words, no visibility), so metatags alone
will not move it. Phase B's intro is the real work.
