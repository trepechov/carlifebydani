## Phase A — Keyphrase research
_Written by `seo-keyphrase-research`._

### What this article is about
A brand-strategy feature built around the Zeekr 7GT's European launch presentation in Málaga.
Two named sources: **Lothar Schüpet** (CEO, Zeekr Europe, ex-BMW) on Zeekr's "technological
premium, not traditional luxury" positioning — cites Forbes research on premium-buyer priorities
(87% want advanced tech, 60% already EV-leaning, 63% open to switching brands) — and **Vincent
Hunneman** (Product Marketing & Pricing) on the 7GT's origin story: conceived summer 2023 when
the European team studied the 007 sedan and concluded the D-segment needed a wagon variant,
built by a dedicated cross-functional European team, launched in China April 2025. Covers
Zeekr's growth formula (Geely Group scale + "European soul" — design HQ in Gothenburg, Sweden,
engineering in Germany/UK/Sweden), European rollout timeline (Nov 2023 NL/SE launch → 2026
"year of acceleration," ~90% EU coverage target), the "Seek More" slogan, 7GT specs (664hp/710Nm,
0–100 in 3.3s, Snapdragon 8295, Mapbox nav), and a deep dive on the **Golden Battery** LFP tech
(faster-charging-than-NMC claim, 10–80% in ~10.5 min per Zeekr, ~9m45s per independent Out of
Spec Reviews tests) — same underlying technology 8950 covers from the service/warranty side
(there as "Golden Brick," this podcast-episode-derived spelling vs. this article's own
"Golden Battery" — same tech, two names, see cannibalisation note below).

  driving-impressions angle (e.g. `Zeekr 7GT първи впечатления` or similar), not bare `Zeekr

- **"Golden Battery" vs. 8950's "Golden Brick" — same underlying tech, two names.** 8950's

  if a reader searches the correct term, both posts are candidates. Checked
  `/wp/v2/search?search=Golden Battery` — only 8913 (this post) uses that exact spelling; 8950
  uses "Golden Brick" throughout its own text. Distinct enough as literal strings not to
  cannibalise today, but noted here in case a future pass wants to reconcile the naming
  site-wide. Not choosing `Golden Battery` as this post's own keyphrase regardless — `Zeekr
  7GT` has the stronger, cleaner autocomplete signal and matches more of the article's actual
  word count (brand strategy + 7GT specs + battery, not battery alone).
