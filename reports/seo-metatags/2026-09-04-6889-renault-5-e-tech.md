### Recommendation
**Focus keyphrase:** `Renault 5 E-Tech` — matches the exact phrase this URL
already ranks organic position 6 for on Google's real SERP, matches the
post's own title, and sits in the middle of both spelling clusters (neither
purely Cyrillic-phonetic nor purely bare-brand). The article genuinely
satisfies this — it's the full model review.
**Secondary:** `Renault 5 electric` (480/mo), `Рено 5` (720/mo, the
Cyrillic-phonetic cluster — real, substantial, distinct search behavior
worth acknowledging in the metadesc even though the title stays in Latin
script matching the brand name), `Renault 5 цена` (90/mo, confirmed price
intent from autocomplete on both spellings).
