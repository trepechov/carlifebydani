### ⚠️ Yoast `wordCount` does NOT count `post_excerpt`

Yoast derives the schema `wordCount` from **`post_content` only**. 154 words in the excerpt
moved it not at all. This corrects the claim in Proposal A that writing `post_excerpt`
lifts `wordCount` from 17 to 150+ — **it does not**.

What this does and does not change:

- ✅ **The text is live, crawlable and in the best position on the page.** Google reads the
  rendered HTML, which is what actually drives ranking and snippet selection. The real win
  is intact.
- ❌ **The `wordCount` property in the Article schema still reads 17**, and **Yoast's own
  content analysis is blind to the text** — so its SEO/readability scores for this post
  remain meaningless.

**To fix `wordCount` too**, the paragraphs have to live in `post_content`. That renders them
in the `col-span-2` column alongside the video rather than full-width above it, so it is a
layout decision, not just a field change. Do **not** put them in both — that duplicates the
text on the page.

### ⚠️ Side effect: the theme auto-links tag names inside content

[`theme/functions.php:75`](../../theme/functions.php#L75) `add_tag_links_to_content` filters
`the_content` and links every post tag where its name appears in the text, **up to 5
occurrences per tag**. It skips `a|td|strong|h2|h3|figcaption` parents but not `<p>`.

Assigning 8 tags therefore injected **10 links to `/tag/` archives inside the 154 words**:

| ¶ | editorial links | auto `/tag/` links |
|---|---|---|
| ¶1 | 2 | 3 |
| ¶2 | 0 | 2 |
| ¶3 | 0 | 5 |

`/tag/blgariya/` ×2 · `/tag/tesla/` ×2 · `/tag/cybertruck/` ×2 · `model-s` · `model-x` ·
`ford` · `supercharger`.

**Why this matters.** It is deliberate theme behaviour built for topical clustering, not a
bug — but it runs against two things already in the backlog: the 2026-08-13 proposal for this
same post said *"No `/tag/` pages as targets — thin taxonomy pages already outrank editorial
content on this site"*, and `/tag/clbd/` absorbs 418 brand impressions at 4 clicks. One link
per 15 words, all pointing at thin archives, also dilutes the two editorial links placed
deliberately — including the one to post 7533, which the backlog wants to rescue.

**Not changed — this is a decision, not a defect.** Options, cheapest first:

1. **Leave it.** The links are internal and do build clusters. But every one of the ~91 posts
   optimized this way will pump ~10 more links into thin tag pages.
2. **Lower the per-tag cap from 5 to 1** in `add_tag_links_to_content` (first mention only) —
   would cut 10 → 7 here, and is the conventional pattern for auto-linking.
3. **`noindex` the thin tag archives** — already an open P1 item in the backlog, and this
   change makes it more urgent rather than less.
