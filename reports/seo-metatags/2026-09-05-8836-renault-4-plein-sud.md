### What this article is about
A genuinely deep, first-person historical piece (**1,651 words**, 10 real H2
headings) about the **Renault 4 E-Tech Plein Sud** — the fabric-roof trim
option on the new Renault 4 E-Tech. Traces every open-roof R4 variant across
65 years: the 1961 factory fabric sunroof option (95×154cm), the 1964
Torpédo Sinpar military prototype, the 1968–70 Plein Air (563 units, sold
in 8 countries), the 1981 Jogging (5,000 units), the 1981–90 JP4/Baja
(~2,000 units, sold as "Renault 4 Frog" in Italy), and the 1985 Sixties
(2,200 units, double glass roof). Then a detailed standard-vs-Plein-Sud
technical comparison (weight +19kg, CdA 0.762→0.778, WLTP range 399→392km,
noise +4% at 130km/h, front headroom +20mm/rear -40mm, opening mechanism:
3 folds instead of 4, polymer not metal) and a genuinely first-person
"Личните ми впечатления зад волана" (my impressions behind the wheel)
section covering morning/midday/evening use, noise at different speeds,
and safety (curtain airbags, anti-intrusion ribs). Available since May 2026
in Techno/Iconic trims, roof option ~€1,800. The richest, most original
content of any post in this cluster.
