**Top Lighthouse issues:** (1) 🔴 Critical — reduce initial server response time

> **Note the field-vs-lab gap:** GTmetrix is a single unthrottled lab test from

> above (real users) passes comfortably (mobile LCP 1.7s, TTFB 0.6s). Field data
> is the ranking signal; treat the GTmetrix TTFB/payload findings as optimization
> targets, not a ranking emergency.
